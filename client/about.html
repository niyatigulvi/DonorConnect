About DonorConnect

DonorConnect is a simple donation platform designed to make it easier for people to donate useful items to those who need them.

Users can donate Clothes, Books and Food. Other users can browse available donations and request the items they need.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce wastage and encourage people to share useful items with others.
